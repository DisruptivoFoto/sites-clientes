Get Started

What to know before your first visit

Getting into care at RTS Bedford is straightforward — here's how it works.

🔓

Direct Access

Under Texas law, you can see a Doctor of Physical Therapy for up to 30 days without a physician referral.

📄

Have a Prescription?

Bring a diagnosis, an "Evaluate and Treat" order, and your physician's signature and date.

🏥

Insurance & Medicare

We coordinate with Medicare and most major insurance plans — call Bedford to verify your benefits first.

About the Bedford Clinic

Part of a 23-year North Texas practice

RTS was founded in 2003 with one purpose: to provide the best in clinical services with an emphasis on a strong client-therapist relationship. Our mission is to deliver outstanding, personalized, evidence-based physical therapy that helps patients regain strength, mobility, and independence. The Bedford clinic is led by Syril John, DPT.

  • ✓Doctorate-level therapists treating musculoskeletal and neuromuscular diagnoses.
  • ✓One-on-one sessions built around your specific diagnosis and goals.
